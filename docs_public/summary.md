# Summary: Database on the evolution of crime in Latin America and the Caribbean during the period 2015-2024, compiled from secondary sources (UNODC, InSight Crime, Prosegur Research, World Bank, ECLAC, UN Women and Infosegura). The dataset includes 230+ record

**Author:** Juan Moisés de la Serna Tuya  
**Institution:** Universidad Internacional de La Rioja (UNIR)  

---

## What is this dataset?

Database on the evolution of crime in Latin America and the Caribbean during the period 2015-2024, compiled from secondary sources (UNODC, InSight Crime, Prosegur Research, World Bank, ECLAC, UN Women and Infosegura). The dataset includes 230+ record

## Why does it matter?

This dataset contributes to open science by making research data freely available for reuse, analysis, and citation.

## Who can use it?

- Researchers and academics
- Policy makers
- Journalists and science communicators
- Students and educators
- General public

## How to use it?

1. Download the data from [GitHub](https://github.com/juanmoisesd/crime-evolution-in-latin-america-2015-2024-database-by-categories)
2. Open with Excel, R, Python or any data analysis tool
3. Cite using the CITATION.cff or CITATION.bib files
